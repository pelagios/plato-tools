Part of PLATO, the Place Attestation Ontology

PLATO tools

Check and convert data about places, in the formats of PLATO. What the formats are, and how to organise your data in them, is explained in the PLATO guide.

Your files stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded: everything happens in this browser tab, and it works for datasets of any size your disk can hold.

Checking many files at once? PLATO tools also runs from the command line, with the same checks and the same reports.

The toolbox

  • Elenchos

    Check

    Put your data to the test: every problem found, named and pointed to, before anyone else relies on it.

  • Metaphrasis

    Convert

    Move your data between PLATO JSON, RDF, the spreadsheet tables and Linked Places Format, and be told exactly what each format cannot carry.

  • Arithmos

    Statistical figures

    Figures from census volumes and statistical tables, published as RDF Data Cube and checked against its rules.

    Choose Convert to N-Triples, with the Data Cube option.
