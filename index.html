Part of PLATO, the Place Attestation Ontology

PLATO tools

Check and convert data about places, in the formats of PLATO. What the formats are, and how to organise your data in them, is explained in the PLATO guide.

Your files stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded: everything happens in this browser tab, and it works for datasets of any size your disk can hold.

Checking many files at once? PLATO tools also runs from the command line, with the same checks and the same reports.

The toolbox

  • Elenchos

    Check

    Put your data to the test: every problem found, named and pointed to, before anyone else relies on it.

  • Metaphrasis

    Convert

    Move your data between PLATO JSON, RDF, the spreadsheet tables and Linked Places Format, and be told exactly what each format cannot carry.

  • Arithmos

    Statistical figures

    Figures from census volumes and statistical tables, published as RDF Data Cube and checked against its rules.

    Choose Convert to N-Triples, with the Data Cube option.

  • Mneme

    Version check

    Published is published. Compare two versions of your dataset and show that nothing was quietly changed or lost.

    Choose the new version, then Compare with the earlier version.

  • Partly here

    Hermes

    Readers

    Bring in the places you linked in Recogito, the places named in a TEI edition, and any spreadsheet (CSV) or GeoJSON you already have. Next: the regions you marked on georeferenced maps.

    Recogito annotations, TEI editions and any CSV or GeoJSON can be read now: you match their columns to PLATO before converting.

  • Agora

    Publish

    From checked to citable: what your dataset still needs to be FAIR, and its deposit files; a permanent address for every attestation; a web page for every place; and the w3id rules that lead to them.

    Choose your dataset, then what to prepare for publishing, and Prepare.

  • Chora

    Place on the map

    See each place on the map, with everything its attestations say of it over time. Draw a point, a line or an area as a new attestation, and save the whole dataset, checked by Mneme.

    Open Chora. Next: trace from georeferenced maps.

  • Krisis

    Match review

    Is this Newton that Newton? Find the same places in another dataset, weigh each suggested match, and keep your judgement, for or against, on the record.

    Choose your dataset, then Match with another dataset. Matching in WHG and other gazetteers is next.
